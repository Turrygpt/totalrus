# Tactical woodland assets

The models listed below are from Sketchfab and licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Both the near and distant variants are adapted from the same source.

| Project files | Source and author |
| --- | --- |
| scots_pine.glb and scots_pine_far.glb | [Scots Pine Trees Set](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/scots-pine-trees-set-422b961ff3d14e7baa7e9077572b2247) by Maksim Batyrev |
| birch.glb and birch_far.glb | [Birch Trees FREE Low Poly](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/birch-trees-free-low-poly-a90b8cca57b44f5492e796cf94d64e80) by Lady Lion Studios / LordSamueliSolo |
| aspen.glb and aspen_far.glb | [Quaking Aspen Trees](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/quaking-aspen-trees-4b5716cb8e3845928e94c93cac6dd526) by Jagobo |
| oak.glb and oak_far.glb | [Oak tree](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/oak-tree-3dc59560f2d24345bdbe65c44636453b) by massive-graphisme |

Adaptations include isolating an individual tree from each pack, adjusting scale and the base pivot, reducing geometry where needed, and baking a distant mesh with a texture atlas from three views.
