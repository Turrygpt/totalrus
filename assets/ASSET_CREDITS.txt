Audio / boot_00.wav through boot_05.wav
Fantozzi's Footsteps (Grass/Sand & Stone) — Fantozzi, single-step edits by qubodup.
https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone
License: CC0 1.0. Six separate Sand footsteps, also intended for grass by the publisher.
Converted to mono 44.1 kHz PCM16, peak -4 dBFS, 2 ms fade-in and 18 ms fade-out.
Source archive, source/license page, preparation script and QC: art/source/audio/infantry_steps.

Audio / hoof_00.wav through hoof_07.wav
SRS_Foley_Horse_Galloping.wav — StephenSaldanha.
https://freesound.org/people/StephenSaldanha/sounds/165532/
License: CC0 1.0. Eight individual hoof contacts cut from the public HQ MP3 preview.
Mono 44.1 kHz PCM16, 0.13-0.15 s, peak approximately -3 dBFS,
2 ms fade-in and 18 ms fade-out. Separate walking and galloping rhythms are driven in-game.
Source recording, source/license page, preparation script and QC: art/source/audio/horse_hooves.
Discovery/reference pack: Horse gallop on different surfaces, congusbongus,
https://opengameart.org/content/horse-gallop-on-different-surfaces
Its archive has mixed CC0 / CC-BY 4.0 licenses; no other pack samples are used in-game.

Horse mesh, rig, and Idle, Walk, Gallop, Death base clips: Quaternius,
Ultimate Animated Animal Pack (July 2021), CC0 1.0.
https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimateanimatedanimals.html
Original file: art/source/horse/Horse.blend. Local additions: Trot, Rear, tack,
mounted pose, rider/spear fall and baked bay coat. The rider and spear use
the existing project assets.
Type 2 uses the user-supplied Tripo horse mesh, rig and PBR textures
from art/source/horse/tripo_horse.glb. The Quaternius motion clips and locally
authored Trot and Rear were transferred to this skeleton. Saddle fit, reins,
rider pose and gallop foreleg adjustment were authored for type 2.
Type 1 seated trot and light galloping seat are animated locally. Motion references:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC1821095/
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19469236/
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27622974/
